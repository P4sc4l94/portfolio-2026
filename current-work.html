My Role within ALR

My part part as a designer in the Creative team involves producing design solutions in key areas:

Building Conversion-Focused Pages

As a web designer I design high-converting landing pages and microsites for specific programs/campaigns, such as our AI Hyper-personalization Student Journeys, Decision IQs, or Paid Search campaigns. I optimize forms, error handling, and trust signals (accreditation, stats, and testimonials), as well as create hierarchy in the design.

Optimize UX, Accessibility, and Performance

I make it a priority to review that our landing pages meet accessiblity compliance as defined by WCAG; we have an established review process that ensures pages respect mobile first, have fast load times, readable typography, inclusive design, and clear navigation. Myself and the other senior web designers built and maintain a component library for consistent, scalable execution.

Support SEO and Content Structure

We have created standardized page templates with proper headings, internal linking, and on-page SEO best practices. These templates are periodically reviewed by our Product, SEO Strategist, and Legal teams to ensure they're compliant with company and marketing standards. Layouts surface keywords naturally and support schema/metadata implementation with engineering and SEO.

Collaborate Across Teams

I regularly work with copywriters, art directors, content strategy leads, product, engineering, QC, and digital strategists to ensure creative, tracking, and handoffs are seamless. In addition, as a senior I provide coaching to the junior designers, as well as conduct cross-team training sessions as needed.

Product Offerings

There are many products that I work on, but the bulk of my work involves building landing pages for Student Journey and design assets for our Conversation Agent and email campaigns.

AI Conversation Agent

The Conversation Agent is an AI assistant that is embedded on our landing pages. It engages prospective adult learners via web chat, SMS, and email to answer questions provide guidance. My responsibility is to design the digital social media ads for the appropriate channels, as well as establish branding for the chat agent and embed it on our pages.

Student Journey

The Student Journey is one of our most popular product offerings. It's a configurable, data-driven lifecycle that maps and automates the path from first interest to application, enrollment, and early onboarding. With it, we deliver personalized, barrier-aware nudges and content across channels (email, SMS, chat, landing pages) triggered by behaviors and milestones. My main role is to collaborate with the Content Strategy Lead and Copywriter to create compelling landing pages and emails that will encourage users to apply.

AI Hyper-personalized Student Journey

Hyper-personalization is an enhancement add-on for Student Journey. It uses machine learning and LLMs to build a live profile of each prospect from CRM, web, chat, and campaign data. Journeys adapt in real time as learners respond to keep messaging accurate, accessible, and compliant. I collaborate with multiple teams, including Engineering, QC, and SEO Strategists to decide on the programs that will be hyper-personalized and then I design and build that dynamic content for our Student Journey landing pages.
