YelpCamp App

2021

This was a project for my Web Development Bootcamp class. The site is an app that utilizes Express, Node.js, and MongoDB (for database hosting). Mapbox is also integrated for the dynamic map. It gives users the ability to create/login to an account, create, rate, or edit/delete campsites and have the content update dynamically.
